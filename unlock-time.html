Unlock time by time zone

Every region unlocks at its own midnight, so the world unlocks in a wave. Each line also shows that zone's midnight in UTC.

  • US Pacific (PST): 19 Nov, 00:00 local = 19 Nov, 08:00 UTC
  • US Eastern (EST): 19 Nov, 00:00 local = 19 Nov, 05:00 UTC
  • UK (GMT): 19 Nov, 00:00 local = 19 Nov, 00:00 UTC
  • Central Europe (CET): 19 Nov, 00:00 local = 18 Nov, 23:00 UTC
  • India (IST): 19 Nov, 00:00 local = 18 Nov, 18:30 UTC
  • Japan (JST): 19 Nov, 00:00 local = 18 Nov, 15:00 UTC
  • Sydney / Melbourne (AEDT): 19 Nov, 00:00 local = 18 Nov, 13:00 UTC
  • New Zealand (NZDT): 19 Nov, 00:00 local = 18 Nov, 11:00 UTC
  • Brazil, Brasilia (BRT): 19 Nov, 00:00 local = 19 Nov, 03:00 UTC
  • Xbox Cloud Gaming (Xbox One / Series): from 1am ET on 19 Nov = 19 Nov, 06:00 UTC; availability may vary by market (Xbox Wire)

US note: the US PlayStation Store lists 05:00 UTC, which is midnight Eastern and 21:00 Pacific on 18 November (PlayStation Store). Rockstar says midnight local. Whether the US west coast unlocks at 21:00 or 00:00 is not confirmed by Rockstar (checked 14 November 2026); be ready at 21:00 Pacific.
