Platforms, editions and preload

What you need to know before you buy, checked 14 November 2026 against Rockstar and PlayStation. Anything without an official source is marked Not yet confirmed.

Platforms

  • Launch platforms: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Not PS4, Xbox One or PC.
  • Release date: 19 November 2026.
  • PC or other later releases: Not yet confirmed
  • Online subscription needed to play: Not yet confirmed

Source: Rockstar Support: Platforms, Editions and Versions.

Editions

  • Standard Edition: $79.99 on the US PlayStation Store.
  • Ultimate Edition: $99.99, digital only. Boxed owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade later.
  • Boxed copies are a download code, not a disc, and are always the Standard Edition.
  • Pre-order bonus: buy before 20 November 2026 to get the Vintage Vice City Pack.

Sources: PlayStation: Grand Theft Auto VI, Rockstar Support. Prices outside the US differ; check your store.

Preload

  • Preload: open now. Digital copies since 10 November (midnight local time on PlayStation, 00:00 GMT on Xbox); boxed codes since 12 November.
  • Space needed on PS5: 212 GB. Clear it before you start the download.
  • Xbox download size: Not yet confirmed

Sources: Rockstar Support, PlayStation.
