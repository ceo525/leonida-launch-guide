Why giving GTA VI is tricky

  • The PlayStation Store has no gift button. On PS5 you give a boxed copy or a PlayStation Store gift card from their country (GTA6.how).
  • The boxed copy is a download code with no disc, and it is always the Standard Edition: Ultimate is digital only (Rockstar Support).
  • PlayStation codes only work on an account from the region they were sold in. Rockstar says Xbox codes are not region-locked (Rockstar Support).
  • Purchases made before 20 November 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack (Rockstar Support).

The card in the kit covers each of these for their console and country.
