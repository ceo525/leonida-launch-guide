How to gift GTA 6 on PS5 (there is no gift button)

Give a boxed copy or a PlayStation Store gift card from their country. The PlayStation Store has no buy-as-gift option, and PS codes only work on an account from the region they were sold in. Sources: Rockstar Support, GTA6.how.

A personalised printable gift certificate plus a how-to-give-it card, so they have something to unwrap. Not a game code. Instant delivery.

The ways that work

  • Boxed copy (easiest to wrap): a download code in a box, no disc, sold from 12 November. It is always the Standard Edition; Ultimate is digital only (Rockstar Support).
  • PlayStation Store gift card: they buy the game themselves, Standard ($79.99) or Ultimate ($99.99) on the US store (PlayStation Store). In the US, two $50 cards cover Standard plus most sales tax (secondary: GTA6.how).
  • Boxed copy + $25 card: covers the $20 Ultimate upgrade if they want Ultimate (upgrade price: Xbox US store; secondary source for PS: GTA6.how).

Region rules (check before you buy)

  • PlayStation game codes only work on an account from the region they were sold in. Xbox codes are not region-locked (Rockstar Support).
  • US and Canadian PS accounts share one region; the UK is its own. A UK code or card will not work on a US account (secondary: GTA6.how).
  • Buy before 20 November 2026 and the copy includes the Vintage Vice City Pack (Rockstar Support). A gift card given later misses it, because they buy after the cutoff.
  • Do not redeem the code yourself: a redeemed code cannot be moved to another account. Keep the receipt until the game is installed.
  • GTA VI is not on PS4, so a PS4-only player cannot use it.
