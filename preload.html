How to start the preload

  • PS5: buy or redeem the game, then turn on auto-downloads and leave the console on or in Rest Mode. Sony's steps: PlayStation support.
  • Xbox Series X|S: buy or redeem the game, turn on automatic updates and leave the console on or in Sleep mode. Microsoft's steps: Xbox support.
  • Boxed copy: it is a download code with no disc. Redeem the code on your account and the preload starts like a digital copy (Rockstar Support).
  • The game unlocks on 19 November 2026. The US PlayStation Store lists 05:00 UTC, which is midnight on the US east coast (PlayStation Store).

Make room for 212 GB

  • PlayStation asks for at least 212 GB of free space on PS5 (PlayStation); Rockstar says the exact size depends on your device (Rockstar Support). Check free space before launch night, not at midnight.
  • Also leave room for patches. The first update, 1.002.000, was about 1.07 GB on PS5 and 2.18 GB on Xbox (reported by KeenGamer, not Rockstar).
  • Delete or move games you are not playing. Saved games stay on the console or in the cloud when you delete a game, so you can reinstall later.
  • The game runs from internal storage or a supported fast SSD. Do not plan to play it from an old USB hard drive.

Preload stuck or not starting?

Check that the console is in Rest Mode (PS5) or Sleep mode (Xbox), not fully off, and that auto-downloads or automatic updates are on. Rockstar points to PlayStation and Xbox troubleshooting pages.
